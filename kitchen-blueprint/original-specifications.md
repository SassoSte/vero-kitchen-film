## Notes for reuse

- The identical cabinet line + Uba Tuba-class granite + black/SS two-tone appliance stack
  appears in #2083 (building sibling) — supports "same kitchen across the complex" for
  unrenovated units; renovated units (#2083 reno, #2077, rental turnarounds) swap finishes
  (Santa-Cecilia-class granite, stone-clad bar, cans, SS appliances) but **not** the layout.
- Model-class language (GE/Whirlpool-class) is deliberate: no nameplate is legible in any
  gallery photo; do not state a brand as fact.
