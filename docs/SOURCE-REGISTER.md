## Known source discrepancies and interpretation

- Kitchen plan's raised-cap stroke is 45 inches; dimensions/specifications/elevation support approximately 60 inches. Use approximately 60 and preserve photographed shape; do not silently redraw the source.
- Whole-unit kitchen area label 142 sqft conflicts with 123×105 inches = **89.6875 sqft**. Do not enlarge the room to reconcile the label. A separate approximate floor-area figure is not a substitute for linear geometry.
- Whole-unit 462-inch label converts to **38 ft 6 in**, not 38 ft 5 in.
- Whole-unit vs living-room sheets use opposite page orientations; schematic DW/stool placement is also unreliable. Source photographs fix camera handedness. Do not mirror the room or move the dishwasher.
- Industry-standard modules, photo estimates and render scaling are different evidence classes. No source here is a survey. Living/dining uncertainty is approximately ±4–6 inches.
- Source prose identifies an exterior/envelope wall at the refrigerator end. It does not establish an appliance-depth recess or validate structure/services. Current flush-wall design is visual intent only.
