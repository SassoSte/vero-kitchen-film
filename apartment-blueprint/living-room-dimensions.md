## Orientation

The sheet draws the balcony on the left and the kitchen bar on the right. No compass
orientation is asserted — the building's wings are mirrored across the community (sibling
#2083 reads mirrored); `unit-plan.svg` uses the opposite convention (balcony on the right,
kitchen on the left). Adopt whichever orientation the walkthrough establishes.
